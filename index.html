01 / The person behind RLumen

Hello, I'm Ronald.

I'm Ronald A. Lalaguna, founder of RLumen — “Illuminating Brand Identities” — a growing creative and operations support studio built to help businesses stay organized and present themselves with confidence.

As a General Virtual Assistant and Visual Designer, I bring together administrative coordination and visual communication. I'm currently developing my digital skills through the DICT SPARK Program and additional technical training.

I hold a Bachelor of Secondary Education, major in English, from Dr. Emilio B. Espinosa Sr. Memorial State College of Agriculture and Technology. My background strengthens the clear communication, organization, and attention to detail I bring to every project.
